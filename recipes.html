Maqluba

A traditional Palestinian rice dish with vegetables and meat, flipped upside down before serving.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1 eggplant, sliced and fried
  • 1 cauliflower, cut and fried
  • 500 g chicken or meat
  • Spices: salt, pepper, cinnamon, allspice

Steps

  1. Boil the meat with spices until cooked.
  2. Fry the eggplant and cauliflower.
  3. Layer the meat and vegetables at the bottom of a pot.
  4. Add the rice and pour the broth over it.
  5. Cook on low heat for about 30 minutes.
  6. Flip the pot onto a large plate and serve.

Hummus

A creamy dip made from chickpeas and tahini.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups boiled chickpeas
  • 1/3 cup tahini
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Salt and olive oil

Steps

  1. Blend the chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and salt.
  2. Add a little water until smooth.
  3. Serve with a drizzle of olive oil.
